# Theme packs

Each pack covers the invitation style, decorations, one craft, one themed game, food twist, cake, goodie bag, and **amazon.se search terms**. Add new packs in the same shape after each party. Children's interests change fast, so confirm the theme every year.

## Contents
- Building a new theme pack
- Stitch / Lilo & Stitch
- Unicorn / magic
- Horses & animals
- Disco / pop star
- Science & slime
- Football / sport
- Mermaid / ocean

## Building a new theme pack
Fill in this skeleton (keep it to about 10 lines):
```
Invitation style:  colours, imagery, one tagline in Swedish
Decor:             3–4 items (banner, balloons in theme colours, tablecloth, cake topper)
Craft (arrival):   one 15-min craft
Game twist:        rename a classic game (skattjakt/stopdans/fiskdamm) to fit the theme
Food twist:        one themed item; keep the rest kid-standard
Cake:              topper or colour idea
Goodie bag:        1 themed item + sweets
amazon.se search:  4–6 exact search phrases
Playlist:          3–5 songs
```

## Stitch / Lilo & Stitch
- **Invitation:** blue, pink and tropical hibiscus. Tagline: *"Ohana betyder familj – välkommen på Stitch-kalas!"*. (No dress-code request; Yelena prefers none.)
- **Decor:** Stitch banner, blue and pink balloons, tropical leaves, flower leis at the door
- **Craft:** decorate Stitch ears on a headband (felt ears + plain headbands)
- **Game twist:** "Hitta Experiment 626" (treasure hunt for hidden Stitch figures), hula stopdans, "sätt öronen på Stitch"
- **Food twist:** pineapple and fruit skewers, blue saft. Hawaiian pizza is optional.
- **Cake:** blue icing + Stitch topper
- **Goodie bag:** Stitch stickers / keyring / mini figure + sweets
- **amazon.se search:** "Stitch kalas dekoration", "Lilo och Stitch ballonger", "Stitch tårtdekoration", "hawaii blomsterkrans barn", "Stitch klistermärken barn", "Stitch figurer set"
- **Playlist:** songs from Lilo & Stitch ("Hawaiian Roller Coaster Ride", Elvis songs from the film)

## Unicorn / magic
- **Invitation:** pastel rainbow + glitter. *"Kom på ett magiskt enhörningskalas!"*
- **Decor:** pastel balloons, rainbow banner, gold stars
- **Craft:** unicorn horn headbands or glitter wands
- **Game twist:** "Fånga enhörningen" (treasure hunt), ballongdans with pastel balloons
- **Food twist:** rainbow fruit skewers, "magic" sprinkles on everything
- **amazon.se search:** "enhörning kalas dekoration", "enhörning diadem barn", "regnbåge ballonger pastell"

## Horses & animals
- **Invitation:** stable, rosettes and hearts. *"Välkommen på hästkalas!"*
- **Craft:** stick horse (käpphäst) decorating, or rosette making
- **Game twist:** käpphästhoppning (stick-horse jumping course), animal charades
- **Option:** pony riding at a local stable (verify price and age limits)
- **amazon.se search:** "käpphäst barn", "häst kalas dekoration", "rosett barn tävling"

## Disco / pop star
- **Invitation:** neon + mirror ball. *"Kom och dansa på discokalas!"*
- **Decor:** disco light, glow sticks, mirror ball
- **Game twist:** stopdans, dance-off, karaoke, "frys som en stjärna"
- **Goodie bag:** glow bracelets, sunglasses
- **amazon.se search:** "discokula barn", "självlysande armband", "karaoke mikrofon barn"

## Science & slime
- **Invitation:** lab coats + bubbles. *"Experimentkalas – kom och forska!"*
- **Craft:** make slime (have it ready-measured; use gloves)
- **Game twist:** volcano (baking soda + vinegar), "mystery box" guessing
- **amazon.se search:** "slime kit barn", "experimentlåda barn", "skyddsglasögon barn"

## Football / sport
- **Invitation:** team jersey design. *"Kom på fotbollskalas!"*
- **Option:** book a sports hall, or a club coach for 1 hour
- **Game twist:** penalty contest, relay races, "medal ceremony" with chocolate coins
- **amazon.se search:** "fotboll kalas dekoration", "medaljer barn", "konor träning"

## Mermaid / ocean
- **Invitation:** turquoise + shells. *"Dyk ner på sjöjungfrukalas!"*
- **Option:** a swimming kalas or a beach kalas in summer (Falsterbo beach)
- **Craft:** shell necklaces, mermaid tail crowns
- **amazon.se search:** "sjöjungfru kalas dekoration", "snäckor pyssel barn"

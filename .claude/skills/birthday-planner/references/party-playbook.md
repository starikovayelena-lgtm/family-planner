## 1. Swedish kalas norms
These are common practice, not fixed rules. Check the class's own norm with the class parents or teacher.
- **Who to invite:** the whole class, all girls (or all boys), or a small group of 2–4. Avoid inviting most of a group but not all of it. Don't hand out invitations at school unless the whole group is invited.
- **When:** send invitations 2–3 weeks ahead, with RSVP about 1 week before. Under 2 weeks still works with a digital invitation and a quick reminder.
- **Length:** 2 hours for ages 4–9, up to 3 hours for ages 10+. Parents usually drop off from about age 6.
- **Presents:** many classes agree on a small budget (often ~100 SEK) or a joint present via Swish. Opening them at the party or later is the family's choice; decide in advance.
- **Family vs friends:** usually two events, a family celebration on the day and the kalas at a weekend.

## 2. Choosing a format
| Format | Group | Parent effort | Rough cost | Best when |
|--------|-------|---------------|-----------|-----------|
| Home + theme | 4–12 | High | 1,000–2,500 SEK | Personal, cheap, ages 4–9 |
| Lekland / play centre | 8–20 | Low | ~200–350 SEK/child | Big group, low effort |
| Trampoline park | 8–20 | Low | ~250–400 SEK/child | Energetic group, ages 6+ |
| Swimming | 6–12 | Medium | varies | Summer or indoor pool. Check swimming ability. |
| Craft / baking / spa | 5–10 | Medium | 800–2,000 SEK | Ages 6–10, calmer groups |
| Cinema / bowling / escape room | 6–12 | Low–medium | varies | Ages 9+ |
| Sleepover (övernattning) | 2–5 | Medium | low | Ages 10+ |
| Outdoor (beach, forest, park) | any | Medium | low | May–Sept only. Needs a rain plan. |

From November to March, assume **indoors**.

## 3. Program templates by age
**Ages 4–6 (1.5–2 h):** arrival play → 1 active game → food → cake → 1 calm game → goodie bags. Short blocks of about 15 min.

**Ages 7–9 (2 h):**
```
+0:00  Arrival: theme craft at the table (gives early arrivals something to do)
+0:20  Active game (themed)
+0:45  Presents (circle) or a second game
+1:00  Food
+1:20  Cake + "Ja, må hon leva" + fyrfaldigt hurra
+1:35  Calm game (fiskdamm / piñata / quiz)
+1:55  Goodie bags at the door
```
**Ages 10–12 (2.5–3 h):** an activity the child chooses (escape room, cinema, baking competition, disco with a karaoke playlist), then food and cake. Less structure, more choice.

Always have **one spare game** plus a calm fallback (a short film or drawing).

## 4. Game bank
- **Active:** stopdans (freeze dance), hela havet stormar, skattjakt (treasure hunt with picture clues for ages under 7), ballongdans, sista man på stolen, dragkamp, limbo
- **Calm:** fiskdamm (a sheet over a doorway with an adult behind it clipping prizes onto the line), Kims lek, piñata, "pin the tail" in the theme, quiz, charades (ages 8+)
- **Crafts:** themed headbands, bracelets, decorating cupcakes, painting a tote bag

## 5. Food, cake & quantities (per child, 2-hour kalas)
- **Mains:** 2 hot dogs **or** 3 slices of pizza **or** 1.5 dl pasta. Allow 20% extra.
- **Sides:** cut fruit/veg about 100 g, popcorn about 1 dl
- **Drinks:** about 3 dl (juice/saft + water)
- **Cake:** 1 slice, plus extra for adults. A cake for 20 slices is enough for 12 kids.
- **Nuts:** make the whole party **nut-free** by default. Ask about other allergies (gluten, lactose, egg) on the invitation.
- **Cake options:** order from a bakery 5–7 days ahead (themed decorations or prinsesstårta), or bake a simple one plus a theme topper from amazon.se.

## 6. Goodie bags (godispåse)
About 25–50 SEK each: a small theme item (sticker sheet, keyring, mini figure) plus sweets. Count = confirmed guests + 1 extra + 1 for the sister. The birthday child usually gets one too, so add 1 more if Yelena wants that. Hand them out **at the door**, not during the party.

## 8. Countdown
**Normal (start 4–5 weeks out):**
- **−5 to −4 wk:** date, format, guest group, theme. Book a venue or entertainer.
- **−3 wk:** invitation sent (Canva + WhatsApp). Wishlist sent to relatives.
- **−2 wk:** order amazon.se items (decorations, crafts, goodie-bag items).
- **−1 wk:** RSVP deadline, then remind non-responders. Final headcount and allergy list. Order the cake.
- **−2 d:** food shopping, assemble goodie bags, test games, make the playlist.
- **−1 d:** practical-info message to parents. Set up decorations.
- **Day:** set up 1 hour early. Brief the adults on their roles.

**Short notice (kalas in under 14 days):**
- **Day 0–1:** pick the date (the nearest weekend at least 8 days away), then send the digital invitation to the class group or by individual message. RSVP in 5–6 days.
- **Day 1–2:** order everything on amazon.se right away. Check the delivery date; if it's tight, buy locally.
- Home format avoids venue booking risk. Keep the theme to decorations, cake and one game.
- Remind non-responders 2 days before the RSVP date.

## 9. Day-of roles & checklist
Roles for 3 adults: **game leader**, **food & cake**, **door & logistics** (guest list, pickups, presents).
```
[ ] Guest list with parent phones, allergies, pickup person (tracker status)
[ ] Games + props + prizes; speaker charged; playlist
[ ] Food, cake, candles, lighter, knife, plates, napkins, bin bags
[ ] Goodie bags (+1 extra, +1 sister)
[ ] Present table + notepad, or log gifts in the tracker as they're opened
[ ] Phone charged for photos (ask about consent before sharing)
```
**Log gifts on the day** (who gave what), so the thank-yous take 5 minutes later.

## 10. Venue leads near Vellinge / Malmö (verify before recommending)
- Malmö stad offers kids' parties at **Folkets Park / Folkets trädgård**. Check current packages on malmo.se.
- Search for: "lekland Malmö kalas", "trampolinpark Malmö kalas", "badhus kalas Malmö/Vellinge", "bowling kalas Malmö", "pyssel kalas Malmö", plus church halls (församlingshem) for renting a room.
- Always verify current price per child, minimum group size, included food, and availability.
